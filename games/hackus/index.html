Rules

Cell Types

  • F = Female
  • PassiveF = Passive Female
  • ExpansiveF = Expansive Female
  • M = Male
  • PassiveM = Passive Male
  • ExpansiveM = Expansive Male

Survival

  • Fewer than 2 neighbors → dies
  • 2 or 3 neighbors → survives
  • More than 3 neighbors → dies

Birth

  • F + M → 1 new cell
  • ExpansiveF + M or F + ExpansiveM → 2 new cells
  • ExpansiveF + ExpansiveM → 4 new cells
  • Expansive births expand outward if nearby dead cells are not enough

Modifiers

  • PassiveF and PassiveM do not participate in reproduction
  • Percentages are applied separately by sex
  • Passive % + Expansive % for each sex must be ≤ 100
